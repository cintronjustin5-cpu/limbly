WHY LIMBLY

Built differently, on purpose.

Curated for quality

Every creator on Limbly is vetted. We review materials, construction, and fit compatibility before anyone can list. No mass-produced knockoffs. No cheap rebrands.

Community-first commerce

The majority of every sale goes directly to the creator. Limbly takes a modest platform fee — just enough to keep the lights on, the curation tight, and the community growing.

By and for the community

Limbly was founded by a bilateral below-knee amputee who got tired of beige. Our advisors, testers, and most of our team are limb-different. We make decisions accordingly.
