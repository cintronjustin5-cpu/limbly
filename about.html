OUR STORY

A home for the makers redefining prosthetics.

Built by and for the limb-different community — a marketplace where exceptional makers and curious customers find each other.

The Story

Limbly started with a question: why is it so hard to find a prosthetic cover that doesn't look like it belongs in a hospital supply catalog?

The founder, Regan Park, is a bilateral below-knee amputee who lost both legs in a climbing accident at 27. After years of navigating a medical system that treated aesthetics as an afterthought — and a consumer market that offered either clinical beige or garish novelty — she started looking for makers who thought differently.

She found them in unexpected places: a ceramicist in Portland who'd been making covers for herself since her amputation at 14. A textile designer in Atlanta incorporating Kente patterns into fabric wraps. A biomedical engineer in Austin who turned her grad school thesis into a product line. A leather craftsman in Denver whose below-knee clients kept asking him to make something for their prosthetics.

These creators existed. They were talented. They were making real things for real people. But they were scattered — hard to find, harder to buy from, and operating without the infrastructure or platform they deserved.

So Regan built one.

Limbly launched in beta in 2024 with 12 creators and roughly 80 products. Within six months, we had 47 creators, over 400 products, and customers in 22 countries. Not because we ran ads or went viral — but because word traveled through communities of people who'd been looking for exactly this.

We remain small by choice. Every creator on Limbly is vetted by a team that includes prosthetists, occupational therapists, and — most importantly — amputees. We care about materials. We care about fit. We care about whether the people making these products have a genuine connection to the community they're serving.

We also care deeply about who this is for. Limbly exists for the person who wants a leg cover that matches their personality, not just their skin tone. For the kid who wants their prosthetic to look like a superhero leg. For the adult who wants theirs to look like art. For anyone who's ever felt like the medical industry saw them as a patient first and a person second.

That's who we're building for. That's who we are.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our values aren't decoration.

These aren't brand adjectives. They're commitments we make to every creator and every customer on Limbly.

Curation over volume

We would rather have 50 exceptional creators than 500 average ones. Quality and community fit matter more than catalog size.

Community first

Limbly is built by people with limb differences, for people with limb differences. That's not a marketing position. It's how we make every decision.

Fair economics

Creators keep the majority of every sale. We charge a platform fee that covers operations and keeps us sustainable — not one that extracts value from the people doing the real work.

Radical transparency

We publish our creator vetting criteria. We're honest about what we don't know. We tell you when something is out of stock instead of taking your money first.

Accessibility always

Every page we build aims for WCAG 2.1 AA. Screen readers are first-class users. We caption every video. We're not done when the design looks good — we're done when everyone can use it.

THE COMPANION MARK

The mark has a story.

The Limbly companion mark isn't a limb. It isn't a person. It's a form in motion — an abstraction of movement, of capability, of the space between what a body is and what it can become.

We worked with a designer who is herself a below-elbow amputee to develop it. The brief was simple: no medical iconography. No literal anatomy. Something that feels like forward motion — confident, specific, alive.

The mark works in two weights: a full-color version for light backgrounds, and a reversed version for dark. It scales from 16px to billboard. It never gets cute about what it represents.

Like everything on Limbly, it's designed to serve the person wearing it — not to make a statement on their behalf.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple. Transparent. Fair.

  1. 01

    Creators apply

    Independent makers apply to join Limbly. We review their work, their materials, their background, and their connection to the community. Acceptance is selective and intentional.

  2. 02

    Products are vetted

    Every product listing is reviewed for accuracy — dimensions, materials, compatibility, and care instructions. We work with the creator to make sure the listing gives buyers everything they need to choose confidently.

  3. 03

    You shop and buy

    Browse by category, prosthetic type, material, or creator. Read real reviews from verified buyers. Purchase securely. Creators fulfill orders directly — no warehouse middleman, no mystery about who made your piece.
