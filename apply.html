JOIN LIMBLY

Apply to sell on Limbly.

Limbly is a curated marketplace — which means we're selective about who sells here. We're looking for exceptional makers who bring craft, care, and genuine community connection to what they make. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

We review every application personally. We typically respond within 2–3 weeks.

What we're looking for.

Exceptional craftsmanship

Your work should demonstrate a high level of skill and care. We look at materials, construction quality, finish, and durability. We're not looking for mass-produced products or white-labeled goods.

Community connection

We strongly prefer makers who are limb-different themselves, or who have a deep and genuine relationship with the limb-different community. This isn't a hard requirement, but it matters to how we evaluate applications.

Fit compatibility focus

Prosthetic accessories need to work with actual prosthetic systems. We expect applicants to have thought seriously about how their products fit, attach, and interact with the range of devices your customers might be using.

Reliable fulfillment

We ask that creators fulfill orders within their stated lead time, respond to customer questions within 48 hours, and maintain accurate inventory. A great product with poor fulfillment is a poor customer experience.

Clear, honest listings

Your listings should include accurate measurements, material disclosures, care instructions, and compatibility information. Customers rely on this to make good purchasing decisions. We won't approve listings that mislead.

Alignment with our values

We expect creators to share our commitment to treating the community with dignity — no inspiration-porn marketing, no ableist framing, no products that demean or tokenize disability. We're building something together.
