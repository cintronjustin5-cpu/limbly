HOW CUSTOM ORDERS WORK

Five steps from idea to delivery.

Custom orders on Limbly are managed through our platform for transparency and protection — but the relationship is directly between you and the creator.

  1. 01

    Browse creators who take custom work

    Not every creator on Limbly accepts custom orders — some focus on their ready-to-ship catalog. Filter the creator page by "Custom Orders" to find makers who are currently open for commissions.

  2. 02

    Submit your brief

    Fill out a brief on the creator's profile — your prosthetic type, measurements, style preferences, timeline, and any reference images. Be as specific as possible. Good briefs lead to great outcomes.

  3. 03

    Review the proposal and pay the deposit

    The creator will review your brief and respond with a proposal: estimated price, timeline, material choices, and any questions. If you accept, you'll pay a 50% deposit to secure your spot in their queue.

  4. 04

    Collaborate through production

    Most creators will share progress photos at key stages. You'll have the opportunity to request minor adjustments before the piece is finished. Major scope changes may require an updated quote.

  5. 05

    Pay the balance and receive your piece

    When the piece is complete, the creator will share final photos for your approval. Once approved, you pay the remaining balance and the piece ships directly to you. Custom orders are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or significantly differ from the approved proposal.

WHAT CAN BE COMMISSIONED

The range is wide.

Custom orders aren't just for unusual sizes. They're for anyone who wants something specific — a pattern, a color palette, a story.

Size variations

Standard products are sized for a range of measurements, but custom orders can be built to your exact circumference, length, and socket interface specs. This is especially valuable for bilateral amputees, pediatric users, or anyone whose measurements fall outside the typical range.

Original designs

Want a cover that tells a specific story — your heritage, your city, a memory, a symbol that matters to you? Describe it in the brief and work with a creator to bring it to life. No two custom pieces are identical.

Material combinations

Custom orders often allow for material combinations that don't appear in the standard catalog — a leather base with a painted ceramic panel, a 3D printed structure with textile insets. Talk to the creator about what's possible within their practice.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Custom order FAQ.

Timelines vary significantly by creator and complexity. A fabric wrap from a creator with open availability might take 2–3 weeks. A hand-sculpted ceramic cover with intricate detail from a creator with a full queue might take 3–4 months. Each creator lists their current estimated lead time on their profile. When you submit a brief, the creator will confirm a specific timeline in their proposal.

Custom orders are made to your specifications, so we generally cannot accept returns unless the piece arrives damaged or is materially different from what was approved in the final photos. That's why we encourage thorough briefs and active participation during the production process. If something goes wrong — damage in shipping, a miscommunication that wasn't caught — contact our support team and we'll work to make it right.

Deposits are held by Limbly until the order is complete and approved. They are not released to the creator until you confirm receipt and approval of the finished piece. If a creator becomes unable to complete an order, your deposit is refunded in full.
