About this piece

The Coral Wave cover is part of Sarah Chen's Coastal Series — a collection inspired by the coastlines of the Pacific Northwest, where she's lived since losing her left leg in a car accident at 14.

Each cover is slip-cast in a food-safe earthenware clay body and hand-painted with underglaze before a final glaze firing. The wave pattern is painted freehand — no two are exactly alike. The matte finish is achieved through a low-temperature top glaze that preserves the hand-painted quality of the design underneath.

The cover attaches via a friction-fit inner sleeve that slides over standard pylon diameters. It's designed to be removed for cleaning and reattached in seconds. The ceramic shell is impact-resistant but not impact-proof — it will survive a dropped laptop but not a hard fall onto concrete.

Sarah tests every design on her own prosthetic before listing it for sale.

Care instructions

  • Wipe the outer surface clean with a soft, slightly damp cloth
  • Do not submerge in water or expose to prolonged moisture
  • Remove before swimming, bathing, or high-sweat activity
  • Store in the included padded sleeve when not in use

MEET THE MAKER

Sarah Chen

Portland, OR · Ceramic & Resin Covers

Sarah is a former occupational therapist who lost her left leg at 14. She trained as a ceramicist in her late 20s and spent years perfecting a process for making covers that are both structurally sound and aesthetically striking.

Her covers have been featured in Disability Arts Online, the Portland Design Week exhibition, and worn by athletes, artists, and everyday people in over 20 countries.
