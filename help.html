Ordering

Browse the shop, select a product, choose any available variants (size, color, finish), and add it to your cart. When you're ready to check out, click the cart icon and follow the checkout flow. You can check out as a guest or create an account for easier order tracking.

You have a 24-hour window after placing an order to request a modification or cancellation. After that, the creator may have already begun production or packaged the item for shipment. Email us at hello@limbly.com as quickly as possible and we'll do our best to help.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also view order status and tracking information in your account under "Orders." If you checked out as a guest, use the tracking number from your email directly with the carrier's website.

Some creators offer gift packaging as an add-on option — you'll see it on the product page if available. We don't offer a site-wide gift wrapping service at this time, but you can add a gift note at checkout and we'll make sure it's included with your order.

Custom Orders

Go to the Creators page and filter for creators who accept custom orders. Visit a creator's profile and submit a brief using their custom order form. Include your prosthetic type, measurements, style preferences, and timeline. The creator will respond with a proposal within a few days.

Custom order pricing varies entirely by creator, complexity, and materials. Most creators list a starting price for custom work on their profile. Final pricing is determined by the creator in their proposal based on your brief. There is no platform markup on custom orders beyond our standard commission.

Custom orders are made to your specifications and are generally not eligible for return. Exceptions apply if the piece arrives damaged or materially differs from the approved design. Deposits are refundable if the creator is unable to complete the order. See our Returns Policy for details.

Sizing & Fit

For transtibial (below-knee) covers, you'll typically need: the circumference at the widest point of the prosthetic (usually mid-calf), the circumference at the narrowest point (above the ankle), and the total length from below the knee joint to the bottom of the pylon. Measurements should be taken over the socket and liner, not the residual limb itself. Each creator's product page includes a sizing chart with specific measurement instructions for their products.

If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for rigid covers (ceramic, 3D printed) and sizing based on the tightest measurement for flexible covers (fabric, silicone). When in doubt, message the creator directly before purchasing — they know their products best and will give you a straight answer about which size works better for your measurements.

Compatibility varies by product. Each listing includes a compatibility table noting which prosthetic systems, pylon types, and attachment methods are supported. If you have a specific system — exoskeletal, endoskeletal, carbon fiber, specific proprietary socket — look for it in the compatibility table or ask the creator. We're working to standardize compatibility information across all listings.

Shipping & Returns

Shipping times vary by creator and destination. Ready-to-ship items typically ship within 1–3 business days; made-to-order items within 1–3 weeks. Domestic US orders generally arrive within 3–7 business days after shipping. International orders typically take 7–21 business days depending on destination and customs processing. Each product page shows the creator's current estimated processing time.

Most creators ship internationally. You'll see a "Ships worldwide" or "Ships from [country]" label on each creator's profile and product listing. Shipping costs and available destinations are shown at checkout. Import duties and taxes for international orders are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the product price.

Ready-to-ship items may be returned within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in original condition. Made-to-order and custom items are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or significantly differ from the product description. To initiate a return, email hello@limbly.com with your order number and reason for return. Return shipping costs are the buyer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error.

Creators

Visit our Apply to Sell page to submit an application. We review all applications and typically respond within 2–3 weeks. We're selective — we look for exceptional craftsmanship, genuine connection to the limb-different community, and products that meet our quality and safety standards.

Limbly takes a 15% platform commission on each sale. Creators keep 85% of the listed price. Payment processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30) are separate and deducted before the creator payout is calculated. There are no monthly fees or listing fees.

Creator payouts are processed on a bi-weekly basis via direct bank transfer or PayPal. Funds are held for 7 days after order confirmation to allow for any return requests, then released in the next payout cycle. New creators have a 30-day holding period on their first payout as a fraud prevention measure.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance across the entire site. All pages include skip-to-content links, semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, ARIA labels on interactive elements, and adequate color contrast. If you encounter a barrier, please report it to accessibility@limbly.com.

Yes. Limbly is tested with NVDA and VoiceOver. All images have descriptive alt text. All interactive elements are keyboard accessible and have meaningful labels. The checkout flow is fully accessible. If you encounter any screen reader issues, contact accessibility@limbly.com.

Email accessibility@limbly.com with a description of the issue, the page or feature where you encountered it, and the assistive technology you were using. We aim to respond within 2 business days and address reported issues as a high priority.

Payment & Security

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay. All transactions are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your full card details on our servers.

Yes. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of certification available. All data is transmitted over encrypted TLS connections. We never have access to your raw card details. See our Privacy Policy for more information on how we handle your data.

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address and applicable local laws. Limbly collects and remits sales tax for US states where we have nexus. International orders may be subject to VAT or import duties assessed by your country's customs authority — these are not included in the checkout price and are the buyer's responsibility.
