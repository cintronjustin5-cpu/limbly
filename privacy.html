Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Limbly, Inc. ("Limbly," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard data when you use our website and marketplace at limbly.com (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you create an account, place an order, apply to be a Creator, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Billing and shipping address
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe; we do not store full card details)
  • Account username and password (stored encrypted)
  • Profile information, including photos, bio, and business details (for Creators)
  • Messages sent through the platform
  • Application materials submitted through the creator application form
  • Reviews and ratings you submit

Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, we automatically collect certain information about your device and interactions, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Pages visited, time on site, and referring URLs
  • Products viewed, added to cart, and purchased
  • Search queries on the site

Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to maintain session state, remember your preferences, analyze site usage, and support payment processing. We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary: Required for the Service to function (session management, cart, authentication). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics: Used to understand how visitors interact with the site (we use a privacy-respecting analytics tool that does not track individuals across sites). You may opt out of analytics cookies in the cookie preference center.
  • Preferences: Used to remember your settings and choices. These are optional.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell your data to advertisers. We do not use Facebook Pixel or Google Ads tracking on this site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including sharing your shipping address with the Creator fulfilling your order
  • Manage your account and provide customer support
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping updates, return confirmations)
  • Send the Limbly newsletter, if you've subscribed — you can unsubscribe at any time
  • Display your reviews and ratings on product and Creator pages
  • Improve the Service through aggregate analytics and user research
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for behavioral advertising, interest-based targeting, or sale to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

With Creators

When you place an order, we share your name, shipping address, and order details with the Creator fulfilling your order. We do not share your payment information or email address with Creators. Creators are contractually obligated to use this information only for fulfilling your order.

With service providers

We use third-party service providers to operate the Service, including:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Shopify — e-commerce platform infrastructure
  • Klaviyo — email marketing (newsletter only, not transactional)
  • Fathom Analytics — privacy-respecting site analytics
  • AWS — cloud infrastructure and storage

These providers are given access to your information only as necessary to perform their services and are bound by data processing agreements.

For legal reasons

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Limbly, our users, or the public.

In a business transfer

If Limbly is acquired, merged with another company, or undergoes a change of control, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you by email before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. Order records are retained for seven years for tax and accounting purposes. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing privacy@limbly.com; some data may be retained as required by law.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link or by emailing privacy@limbly.com.
  • Object / Restrict: Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is sold or disclosed and to whom, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

EEA, UK, and Swiss residents have rights under the GDPR and equivalent legislation. Our legal basis for processing your data is generally: performance of a contract (order fulfillment), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, service improvement), and consent (newsletter marketing).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@limbly.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including TLS encryption for all data in transit, encrypted storage of sensitive data, and access controls limiting employee access to personal data on a need-to-know basis. However, no system is perfectly secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account and to enable two-factor authentication if offered.

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at privacy@limbly.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. International Transfers

Limbly is based in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer. For transfers from the EEA to the US, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and notify registered users by email. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact
